# Review of the modular-architecture plans (01, 02, 03)

Reviewed against the source on `refactor/modular-architecture` (343 `.ts` files under `core/`, `extensions/`, `test/`), Biome 2.5.15 and npm 11.19.1 behaviour checked locally. Line references are to the plan files and to the current source.

## Verdict

**Rework.** Plan 02 is the right shape (layout, rule set, phase order, split flags) and most of its facts hold, but three things would break behaviour or the published package as written, and its own rule R7 is contradicted by its own migration tables in roughly twenty places. The three plans also disagree on the test-framework layout and on eval plumbing; the reconciled decisions in section 3 settle each one. Once findings 1 to 4 are folded into plan 02 and the biome.json of plan 01 is extended per finding 9, the plan can be approved.

## 1. Findings

Severity: **blocker** = breaks behaviour, the package or `npm run check`; **major** = the plan cannot be executed as written or contradicts itself; **minor** = wrong fact or naming.
